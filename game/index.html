Before you play — what this is, and what it isn't

Markets & Makers is a game. Everything you earn inside it — Merc Dollars, $MM, businesses, upgrades — is part of the game and currently stays inside it. $MM cannot be withdrawn, sold, or exchanged for anything outside Mercedonia, and whether it ever will be is undecided.

This is not an investment. There is no return, no yield, no redemption, and no promise of profit. Do not spend money on the expectation of getting money back — there is no mechanism by which you could.

Your progress lives in this browser. It is stored locally. Clearing your site data, switching browsers, or using a different device will lose your city. Signing in with a wallet identifies you to the shared world; it does not back up your game.

Your wallet. Signing in proves you hold an address by signing a message. It never moves funds, never requests a transaction, and never asks for a seed phrase or private key. We will never ask you for one.

You must be 18 or older to sign in with a wallet. The demo has no such requirement, because it touches nothing outside your browser.

This is a prototype. The world is under active development. Economies get rebalanced, features change, and progress may be reset.
