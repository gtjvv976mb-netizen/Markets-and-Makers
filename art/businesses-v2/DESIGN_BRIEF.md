# Solarpunk business redesign brief

## Shared language

The set uses warm mineral plaster, weathered teal, ochre/copper, timber, cyan water/glass, dark photovoltaic surfaces, and living green. Sustainability is shown as working infrastructure—energy collection, rain capture, water treatment, material recovery, passive ventilation, and repair loops—not as unrelated greenery pasted onto roofs.

Every model is composed around one strong roofline or massing idea and one oversized occupational prop. This keeps the trade recognizable when the game renders a building at roughly thumbnail scale on a phone.

| Business | Unique silhouette | Trade cue | Regenerative system |
|---|---|---|---|

## Reference provenance

Primary precedents used to translate real regenerative architecture into low-poly game form:

- Water treatment as public architecture: [BNIM · Omega Center for Sustainable Living](https://www.bnim.com/projects/omega-center-for-sustainable-living/)
- Greenhouse/biome structure: [Grimshaw · Eden Project Biomes](https://grimshaw.global/projects/culture-and-exhibition/the-eden-project-the-biomes/)
- Microgrid components and resilience: [NREL · Microgrids for Anyone](https://www.nrel.gov/news/features/2022/microgrids-for-anyone.html)
- Electric underground mining: [Epiroc · Underground electrification](https://www.epiroc.com/en-us/products/electrification-solutions/underground-electrification)
- Sawline process: [Wood-Mizer · System 3](https://woodmizer.com/us/systems/system-3)
- Fabrication-lab legibility: [Fab Foundation · Getting Started](https://fabfoundation.org/getting-started/)
- Manufacturing hall precedent: [Foster + Partners · McLaren Production Centre](https://www.fosterandpartners.com/projects/mclaren-production-centre/)
- Off-site construction: [Laing O'Rourke · Explore Manufacturing](https://www.laingorourke.com/company/our-businesses/explore-manufacturing/)
- Electric freight/shore power: [Port of Rotterdam · RWG shore-based power](https://www.portofrotterdam.com/en/news-and-press-releases/rotterdam-world-gateway-container-terminal-invests-in-shore-based-power)
- Community food/hospitality: [WOHA · Kampung Admiralty](https://woha.net/project/kampung-admiralty/)
- Open bamboo sports hall: [Panyaden campus](https://www.panyaden.ac.th/campus/)
- Solar cinema cue: [The Sol Cinema](https://www.thesolcinema.org/)
- Material recovery as civic architecture: [Selldorf Architects · Sunset Park Material Recovery Facility](https://www.selldorf.com/projects/sunset-park-material-recovery-facility)

Mobile asset decisions follow the [Khronos real-time asset creation guidelines](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/3DC-Asset-Creation/blob/main/asset-creation-guidelines/RealtimeAssetCreationGuidelines.md). The runtime remains one opaque vertex-colour draw call per building because reducing material/primitive count matters more here than adding texture detail.
